# KeshavaSon

**Practical tools for responsible AI and public benefit.**

I am developing tools that help teams explain what they built, what they checked, what remains uncertain and who can decide the next step. The aim is useful software that people can inspect, question and improve.

This work is informed by [MiTrabajadorIA’s methodology](https://mitrabajadoria.es/metodologia). It turns ethical commitments into questions about a specific release and the people affected by it. No religious affiliation is required to use or challenge the work.

## What guides the work

- **Evidence:** distinguish intentions, observations and tested results; keep sources, versions and unresolved questions visible.
- **Privacy and autonomy:** use only the data needed, protect people's choices and consider who bears the costs.
- **Accountability:** make purpose, scope, authority and responsibility explicit before consequential actions.
- **Repair:** record mistakes, correct their effects and improve the process without hiding its history.

## Current work

**satya-review** is a local prototype for a readable Spanish/English release review, linked to the reviewed files. It helps document eight practical questions and detect later file changes. Public release is in preparation.

Its records do not authenticate reviewers or certify ethics, security or legal compliance. The usefulness of this approach still needs feedback from external users.

## Collaborate

I welcome engineers, researchers and practitioners from businesses, civil society and public institutions who want to test these ideas against real constraints.

**What evidence would your team need before trusting an AI-assisted release—and what would make you stop it?**

Useful contributions include reproducible examples, failure cases, accessibility improvements and criticism that makes the work clearer or simpler. Start a conversation through [MiTrabajadorIA](https://mitrabajadoria.es).

## Transparency

AI assists this work, including drafting, coding and review. An AI review is not independent human approval. Project documentation should state what was actually checked, by whom, and what remains open. I do not claim certification, institutional endorsement or proven impact without supporting evidence.
